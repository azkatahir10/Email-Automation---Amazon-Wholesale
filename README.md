# Amazon Wholesale Email Automation

**Automate Your Supplier Outreach. Save Time. Focus on Growing Your Amazon Business.**

A Python-powered email automation solution designed specifically for **Amazon Virtual Assistants (VAs)** who handle wholesale sourcing and brand outreach.

Managing supplier outreach manually can be repetitive and time-consuming. This tool simplifies the process by automating personalized wholesale email draft creation directly in Gmail.

### Key Features
- **Automated Email Draft Generation:** Create multiple supplier outreach drafts automatically.
- **Personalized Emails:** Automatically incorporate brand names, ASINs, product descriptions, and supplier contact details.
- **Gmail Integration:** Generate drafts directly in your Gmail account.
- **Duplicate Prevention:** Avoid processing the same leads repeatedly.
- **Supplier Contact Management:** Validate email addresses and organize supplier information.
- **Contact Form Support:** Generate separate, ready-to-use messages for suppliers who only provide website contact forms.
- **Bulk Lead Processing:** Process multiple supplier leads through a simple CSV file.
